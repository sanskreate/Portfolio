Solo build · Product, frontend & AI

Audiocular — sound tuned to your ears.

A web app that talks straight to DAC/IEM hardware from the browser, measures how you hear, and recommends EQ that fits you. I designed and built it end to end, on my own.

  • Hardware in the browser: WebHID connects DAC/IEM devices directly; the PEQ editor draws live graphs across 5 filter types.
  • A hearing test that means something: a clinical-style 7-frequency audiogram that runs in the browser and converts dBFS thresholds to dB HL, then compensates with EQ.
  • "Listeners like you": a GMM similarity model over filter curves recommends presets, alongside genre-blended EQ.
